# Every closed Hankel channel normal-orders into the leakage ideal

## 1. Result (L272, 2026-07-25)

The ideal-placement stop condition in A213 is exact.

Retain L236/L255's two Hardy isometries and cross Gram

\[
\mathscr H=\mathcal O_R\mathcal O_L^*,\qquad
\mathscr H_{n,j}=B_{n+j}^*.
\]

Let \(L\) be the forward Hardy shift and put

\[
\widehat{\mathcal O}_L=L^*\mathcal O_L,\qquad
\widehat{\mathscr H}
=\mathcal O_R\widehat{\mathcal O}_L^*
=\mathscr H L.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{L^*\widehat{\mathscr H}
=\widehat{\mathscr H}L}                           \tag{1}
\]

and, by L254,

\[
\boxed{
\widehat{\mathscr H}^*\widehat{\mathscr H}
=P_{\mathcal K_B}
=I-\mathcal L_B,\qquad
\mathcal L_B=\mathcal T_B\mathcal T_B^*.}         \tag{2}
\]

Consequently every normally ordered copy-scalar shift word obeys

\[
\boxed{
\widehat{\mathscr H}^*
L^a(L^*)^b
\widehat{\mathscr H}
=(L^*)^a(I-\mathcal L_B)L^b.}                    \tag{3}
\]

The unshifted version is

\[
\boxed{
\mathscr H^*L^a(L^*)^b\mathscr H
=(L^*)^a(I-L\mathcal L_BL^*)L^b.}                \tag{4}
\]

Thus a closed doubled-Hardy block word containing at least one
transfer channel differs from its copy-free half-line word by an
element of the two-sided leakage ideal

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak J_B
=\langle\mathcal L_B\rangle_{\mathcal A},}        \tag{5}
\]

where \(\mathcal A\) is the copy-scalar algebra generated by
\(L,L^*\) and finite row projections.  Here \(\mathfrak J_B\)
allows several leakage factors; it is not being silently identified
with the single-sandwich space
\(\mathcal A\mathcal L_B\mathcal A\).

Applied coefficientwise to L255's complete physical pencil and
L258's closed renewal, this proves:

1. no one-sided \(B^\sharp\) term survives as a scalar contribution;
2. no copy-dependent renewal factor remains outside the leakage
   ideal; and
3. for a complete delay \(k=r+1\ge2\), every term at the active
   coefficient \(c^{2k}\) has at most one non-background leakage
   selection and therefore has trace form
   \[
   \operatorname {tr}
   \{\mathcal L_B\Phi(L,L^*)\mathcal L_B\},
   \qquad \Phi\in\mathcal A.                       \tag{5a}
   \]

This closes A213's first stop condition.  It does **not** prove A194:
the remaining task is to normal-order the copy-scalar return, isolate
the depth-\(k+1\) divergence found by L269, and prove that every other
boundary correction is shallow enough for L266.  L271's full-face
support theorem is an equivalent concrete target for that remaining
depth calculation.

## 2. The single-sandwich identity

Both Hardy maps are isometries.  Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathscr H^*\mathscr H
&=\mathcal O_L\mathcal O_R^*
  \mathcal O_R\mathcal O_L^*
 =\mathcal O_L\mathcal O_L^*
 =P_{\mathcal K_{zB}}.
\end{aligned}                                    \tag{6}
\]

Applying the backward shift to L254's exact model split gives (2).
The Hankel entries of the shifted cross Gram are

\[
\widehat{\mathscr H}_{n,j}=B_{n+j+1}^*,
\]

so shifting a row down or a column right proves (1).
Taking adjoints gives

\[
\widehat{\mathscr H}^*L
=L^*\widehat{\mathscr H}^*.                      \tag{7}
\]

Repeated use of (1) and (7) yields

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\mathscr H}^*
L^a(L^*)^b\widehat{\mathscr H}
&=(L^*)^a
  \widehat{\mathscr H}^*\widehat{\mathscr H}
  L^b,
\end{aligned}
\]

and (2) proves (3).  The same argument with (6), together with

\[
P_{\mathcal K_{zB}}=I-L\mathcal L_BL^*,
\]

proves (4).

Every finite copy-scalar word reduces under \(L^*L=I\) to
\(L^a(L^*)^b\), so (3)--(4) apply word by word.  Fixed row
projections cause no exception because
\(P_j=L^j(I-LL^*)(L^*)^j\) belongs to \(\mathcal A\).

## 3. Closed-cycle induction

L255 writes every entry of the doubled physical pencil using:

1. copy-scalar shifts and row projections;
2. linear crossings \(\mathscr H,\mathscr H^*\); and
3. one already-paired quadratic term
   \(\mathscr H P_0L^*\mathscr H^*P_0\), up to the displayed
   orientation convention.

Holomorphic functional calculus expands coefficientwise into the
same block-word algebra.  The metric inverse and L258's renewal
inverse also have invertible copy-scalar constant terms, so every
fixed coefficient is a finite sum of such words.

A scalar trace path is closed.  Therefore every crossing from one
Hardy copy to the other is paired with an opposite crossing.  Choose
an innermost pair.  Its intervening word is copy-scalar, so (3) or
(4) replaces the pair by

\[
\text{copy-free word}
-\text{a word in }\mathcal A\mathcal L_B\mathcal A.
\]

Iterate over the remaining pairs.  Expanding the resulting product
shows that the complete word is its all-copy-free counterpart plus
terms each containing at least one \(\mathcal L_B\).  Those terms
belong to \(\mathfrak J_B\).  At this stage a term may contain
several leakage projections, so it would be incorrect to claim yet
that the operators between them are copy-scalar.

The copy-free cancellation used here is the exact background
cancellation in L258 Section 3: at the half-line background
\(R_PZ_{\rm ret}=I_P\), and hence the word-free part of
\(\mathfrak D_{\rm ret}=I_P-R_PZ_{\rm ret}\) vanishes before
coefficient extraction.  This is stronger and more precise than
formally setting \(\mathscr H=0\) in one block of L255.

### Product-level delayed filtration

It remains to justify the single-sandwich assertion (5a).  Put
\(r=k-1\ge1\).  L243 writes every retained physical resolvent using
one delayed inverse-model kernel and proves the exact valuation table

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{nonconstant inverse-kernel powers}
&\text{least total }c\text{-degree}\\ \hline
1&2r+1\\
p\ge2&(p+1)r+p\ge3r+2 .
\end{array}                                       \tag{8}
\]

The second line is already strictly above the active degree:
\(3r+2>2r+2=2k\).  Two first nonconstant selections arising in
different factors have degree at least
\(2(2r+1)=4r+2>2r+2\).  The remaining ingredients cannot undo this
count:

1. L245's chain Green columns have nonnegative \(c\)-valuation;
2. the direct-map scalar coefficients and the boundary-metric
   coefficients have nonnegative valuation; and
3. L258's renewal inverse has identity constant term because
   \(Z_{FF}(0)=0\), so renewal never lowers valuation.

L243's split, for \(\mathcal C(u)=u^r\mathcal B(u)\),
\[
K_{\mathcal C}=\sum_{j<r}t^jI+t^rK_{\mathcal B},
\]
identifies each such nonconstant model-kernel selection with one
non-background closed transfer pair.  Under L255's doubled-Hardy
realization, normal ordering that pair uses (2) and contributes the
\(-\mathcal L_B\) part of \(P_{\mathcal K_B}=I-\mathcal L_B\).
Thus the active coefficient contains at most one leakage projection
after its copy-free counterpart is removed.  Every active scalar
trace is consequently
\[
\operatorname {tr}\{A\mathcal L_BC\}
=\operatorname {tr}
\{\mathcal L_BCA\mathcal L_B\},\qquad A,C\in\mathcal A, \tag{9}
\]
where the last equality follows from cyclicity and
\(\mathcal L_B^2=\mathcal L_B\).  This proves (5a).

The case \(k=1\), for which \(r=0\) and the delayed filtration is
inapplicable, is the exact universal base calculation L256.

This proves the three placement conclusions in Section 1.  It does
not prove the lower vanishings or bound the unilateral boundary
depth of \(\Phi\); those are the remaining theorems.
