# The finite prepared columns admit endpoint-null delay gauges

## 1. Result (L284, 2026-07-25)

Retain the balanced pure partial isometry

\[
I-S^*S=VV^*=:E,\qquad I-SS^*=WW^*=:F,\qquad
Q=I-E,
\]

and let \(C_4,C_5,C_6\) be the prepared defect-factor columns from
L232--L234.  There are explicit polynomial columns
\(N_4,N_5,N_6\), each perpendicular to \(V\), with two properties.

First, each \(N_r\) is a **global endpoint-null gauge**:

\[
\boxed{
V^*\mathcal G_S(VN_r^*+N_rV^*)V=0,\qquad
W^*\mathcal G_S(VN_r^*+N_rV^*)W=0.}               \tag{1}
\]

Second, the normalized columns

\[
\boxed{\widetilde C_r=C_r-N_r}                    \tag{2}
\]

vanish on the relevant complete-delay stratum:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde C_4&=0 &&\text{if }B_1=0,\\
\widetilde C_5&=0 &&\text{if }B_1=B_2=0,\\
\widetilde C_6&=0 &&\text{if }B_1=B_2=B_3=0.
\end{aligned}}                                    \tag{3}
\]

Thus each existing preparation can, **through its own order**, be
made compatible with exact delay removal without changing either
endpoint response.  The dimension-free coefficient bounds are

\[
\boxed{
\|\widetilde C_4\|\le25,\qquad
\|\widetilde C_5\|\le122,\qquad
\|\widetilde C_6\|\le\frac{851}{2}.}               \tag{4}
\]

L284 does not yet give a simultaneous all-series gauge.  Changing
\(C_4\) changes the recomputed fifth and later coefficients, and
changing \(C_5\) changes the recomputed sixth and later coefficients.
Those transports must be included in an induction.  The theorem
removes a narrower obstruction: the nonzero delayed values of the
individual L232--L234 columns are gauge, not compulsory physical
data.  A direct rank-chain audit confirms the warning: subtracting
the fourth- and fifth-order gauges simultaneously while reusing the
stored later columns leaves a fifth-flag residual of norm
\(5.99\times10^{-3}\).

## 2. A general endpoint-null certificate

The construction is elementary.  Let \(Z=Z^*\) be a polynomial in
\(S,S^*\), put

\[
Y=Z-S^*ZS,\qquad P=QYE,                            \tag{5}
\]

and assume

\[
\boxed{
EZE=FZF=0,\qquad EYE=QYQ=0.}                      \tag{6}
\]

Since \(I=E+Q\), equations (5)--(6) imply

\[
Y=P+P^*.                                           \tag{7}
\]

Write \(P=NV^*\), equivalently \(N=PV\).  Then \(V^*N=0\), and
purity makes the Stein series telescope:

\[
\mathcal G_S(VN^*+NV^*)
=\mathcal G_S(Y)=Z.                                \tag{8}
\]

Compressing (8) by \(V\) and \(W\) proves (1).  This converts the
search for an endpoint-null column into finite polynomial identities;
no response inverse or selected kernel frame occurs.

## 4. Exact delay divisibility

Let \(\mathcal I_d\) be the two-sided partial-isometry word ideal
generated by the first \(d\) transfer-delay relations

\[
ES^jF=0,\qquad F(S^*)^jE=0,\qquad 1\le j\le d.
\]

Exact quotient reduction gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
(C_4V^*-P_4)&\in\mathcal I_1,\\
(C_5V^*-P_5)&\in\mathcal I_2,\\
(C_6V^*-P_6)&\in\mathcal I_3.
\end{aligned}}                                    \tag{14}
\]

Evaluation on a completely delayed colligation proves (3).  Direct
coefficient counting in the normalized lifts gives the bounds (4).

The exact certificate checks, in each order:

1. Hermiticity of \(Z_r\);
2. all four corner/support identities in (6);
3. \(Y_r=P_r+P_r^*\);
4. \(E(C_rV^*-P_r)=0\); and
5. the appropriate quotient identity in (14).

Every residual has zero words.

## 5. Consequence for A178

L232 noted that its quartic column was nonzero but endpoint-null on a
complete delay, and L233--L234 inherited the same gauge issue.  L284
shows that this is not an unavoidable failure of delay covariance:
each finite preparation has a globally endpoint-null representative
whose normalized version is delay-divisible.

The remaining induction has a precise extra term.  After normalizing
order \(r\), the state metric changes by \(-Z_r\); the forcing at
orders \(r+1,r+2,\ldots\) must be transported before the next
normalization is chosen.  A valid recursion must prove that those
transport terms remain in the preceding right ideal and keep a
summable coefficient bound.  One may not normalize all three stored
columns independently and then reuse L233--L234's later coefficients
unchanged.
