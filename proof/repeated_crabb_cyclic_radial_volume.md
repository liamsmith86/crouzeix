# The delayed volume target is one last radial moment

## 1. Result (L252, 2026-07-25)

Retain a finite pure partial isometry with orthogonal defects

\[
I-S^*S=VV^*=:E,\qquad I-SS^*=WW^*=:F,\qquad EF=0,
\]

and put

\[
B_j=W^*(S^*)^jV,\qquad
Q_j=(S^*)^jS^j,\qquad n=\dim{\cal H},\quad
m=\operatorname {rank}E=\operatorname {rank}F .
\]

Then every radial power trace has the exact transfer-energy expansion

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname {tr}Q_j
=n-jm+\sum_{h=1}^{j-2}(j-h-1)\|B_h\|_F^2
\qquad(j\geq0),}                                  \tag{1}
\]

where an empty sum is zero.  In particular, under the complete delay

\[
B_1=\cdots=B_{k-1}=0,                             \tag{2}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname {tr}Q_j&=n-jm &&(0\leq j\leq k+1),\\
\operatorname {tr}Q_{k+2}
&=n-(k+2)m+\|B_k\|_F^2 .
\end{aligned}}                                    \tag{3}
\]

Consequently the remaining A194 volume law is equivalent to one
particularly small cyclic normal-form assertion.  If L250's
edge-deleted normalized mass satisfies

\[
\boxed{
[c^{2k}]
\left\{-m+\operatorname {tr}(N^{-1}H)\right\}
=4\operatorname {tr}
\{Q_{k+2}-(k+2)Q_1+(k+1)I\}}                     \tag{4}
\]

modulo the trace ideal generated by the lower delay relations
\(B_1=\cdots=B_{k-1}=0\), then

\[
\boxed{
[c^{2k}]
\left\{-m+\operatorname {tr}(N^{-1}H)\right\}
=4\|B_k\|_F^2.}                                   \tag{5}
\]

Thus the paired-channel calculation left by L251 need not produce a
positive matrix factor or cancel every state word separately.  It is
enough to prove the cyclic congruence (4): after trace reduction all
nonradial closed channels must lie in the lower-delay trace ideal, the
top radial coefficient must be \(4Q_{k+2}\), and the two affine
baseline moments must be the displayed \(Q_1\) and \(I\) terms.

Equation (1), and hence the implication (4) \(\Rightarrow\) (5), is
proved for every grade.  Equation (4) is still open in arbitrary
grade.  Exact noncommutative cyclic reduction verifies it through
grades one to six.  In that audit the Stein face and the final-row
whitening correction are also reduced separately: every nonradial
cyclic class cancels coefficientwise between those two pieces.  This
finite cancellation pattern exposes the role expected of L244's
coisometry, but it does not prove the all-grade congruence.

## 2. Two telescopes

Set

\[
g_j=\operatorname {tr}\{S^jF(S^*)^j\}
=\|S^jW\|_F^2 .
\]

Since \(SS^*=I-F\), cyclicity gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname {tr}Q_j-\operatorname {tr}Q_{j+1}
&=\operatorname {tr}
\{S^jS^{*j}-S^{j+1}S^{*(j+1)}\}\\
&=\operatorname {tr}\{S^jF(S^*)^j\}
=g_j.                                             \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]

The loss from one left-defect orbit to the next is

\[
\begin{aligned}
g_j-g_{j+1}
&=\operatorname {tr}
\{W^*(S^*)^j(I-S^*S)S^jW\}\\
&=\|V^*S^jW\|_F^2
=\|B_j\|_F^2.                                     \tag{7}
\end{aligned}
\]

Here \(g_0=m\) and \(B_0=W^*V=0\).  Therefore

\[
g_j=m-\sum_{h=1}^{j-1}\|B_h\|_F^2.               \tag{8}
\]

Sum (6) from \(0\) to \(j-1\), insert (8), and use
\(\operatorname {tr}Q_0=n\).  This gives exactly (1).
Specializing (1) under (2) proves (3).

Finally,

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname {tr}
\{Q_{k+2}-(k+2)Q_1+(k+1)I\}\\
&\quad=
\{n-(k+2)m+\|B_k\|_F^2\}
-(k+2)(n-m)+(k+1)n\\
&\quad=\|B_k\|_F^2,
\end{aligned}
\]

which proves the all-grade implication (4) \(\Rightarrow\) (5).

## 3. Why this is the right quotient

Before cyclic reduction, the coefficient in (4) is large: even at
grade four it contains thousands of reduced state words.  Trace
cyclicity exposes further partial-isometry reductions that are
invisible in a fixed word normal form.  For example,

\[
\operatorname {tr}\{S^*S^5\}
=\operatorname {tr}\{SS^*S^4\}
=\operatorname {tr}S^4,
\]

because \(FS=0\).  Such identities remove the apparently nonradial
remainders rather than assigning them artificial endpoint weights.

After the complete-delay relations are imposed, the exact reductions
through grade six give

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
k&
[c^{2k}]\{-m+\operatorname {tr}(N^{-1}H)\}\\ \hline
1&4\operatorname {tr}(Q_3-3Q_1+2I)\\
2&4\operatorname {tr}(Q_4-4Q_1+3I)\\
3&4\operatorname {tr}(Q_5-5Q_1+4I)\\
4&4\operatorname {tr}(Q_6-6Q_1+5I)\\
5&4\operatorname {tr}(Q_7-7Q_1+6I)\\
6&4\operatorname {tr}(Q_8-8Q_1+7I).
\end{array}                                       \tag{9}
\]

The coefficient pattern is not being extrapolated as a proof.
Rather, (9) identifies the precise arbitrary-grade statement that
the L251 paired-channel trace must establish.  L244 supplies the
word-free coisometric cancellation; L251 prevents a one-sided
\(\mathcal B^\sharp\) term; (4) is the remaining cyclic reduction
of the closed pairs.  The component audit adds a sharper diagnostic:
at grades one through six, every cyclic class outside the three
radial classes occurs with opposite coefficients in the raw Stein
face and in the final-row whitening correction.  Thus the
coefficientwise cancellation is not merely a late comparison with
the transfer target.
