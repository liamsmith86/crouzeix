\[

\]

with \(D\) given by (2).  Since

\[
{\cal H}(0)=E,\qquad \Gamma(0)=I,

Moreover,

\[
\boxed{

\{P_{\rm bl}V-A^*P_{\rm bl}AV\}\Gamma^{-1/2}.}    \tag{9}

## 3. The retightening column is physically rooted

L298 writes

\[
\begin{aligned}
C_k
={}&-\frac12V B_k^*B_k\\
&-\frac12Q\left\{
 S^kWB_k+
 \sum_{j=1}^{k-1}(S^*)^{k-j}VB_k^*B_j
\right\}.                                        \tag{10}
\end{aligned}
\]

The defect identities give

\[
\begin{aligned}
VB_k^*B_kV^*&=G_kG_k^*,\\
S^kWB_kV^*&=S^kG_k^*,\\
VB_k^*B_jV^*&=G_kG_j^*.
\end{aligned}                                     \tag{11}
\]

Substitution proves (4).  In particular, multiplying \(C_kV^*\)
on either side by any finite word preserves membership in the

Because \(V^*V=I\),

\[
C_k=(C_kV^*)V,\qquad C_k^*=V^*(C_kV^*)^*.        \tag{12}
\]

Write \(H(c)=\sum_{n\ge1}c^nH_n\).  The coefficient of (6) with

\[
r_kc^{n+2k}\left\{
(H_nV^*)(C_kV^*)^*
(C_kV^*)(H_nV^*)^*
\right\}.                                        \tag{13}
\]

By Section 2, \(H_nV^*\) is a finite physical polynomial.  Equations
(4) and (13) show directly that (13) is a finite Hermitian bridge

## 4. Weighted L311 normalization

Apply L311 to every rooted word in (13).  If its total coefficient
degree is

\[
d=n+2k,
\]

then its literal endpoint factor contains

\[
c^dB_k
=c^{d-k}(c^kB_k),\qquad d-k=n+k\ge0.             \tag{14}
\]

L311's response bound is linear in \(\|B_k\|_F\), with a constant
linear in the finite prefix/suffix word lengths.  Multiplication by
\(c^d\) gives

\[
c^d\|B_k\|_F
=c^{d-k}\|c^kB_k\|_F
\le c^k\|c^kB_k\|_F                              \tag{15}
\]

for \(0<c\le1\) and \(n\ge1\).  There are only finitely many terms
through a fixed jet, so summing (14)--(15) proves (7) and its stated
bounds.  L311 also puts every selected response lift in the same
bridge ideal, proving complete-delay normalization.

The factor square \(C^*C\) is even better rooted, but L307's

kept intact rather than decomposed by this lemma.
