## 2. Concrete logical gap: which variables have been optimized?

L351, equation (9), concerns the tracking response after optimizing
**both the physical disk fibre and all zero motions at fixed shape**:

\[
 \tau_{\mathrm{full}}(a)=R\overline a.
\]

L355, equation (12), concerns optimization of **zero motions only, at
fixed physical direction**:

\[
 \overline{\tau_{\mathrm{zero}}(C)}=-T^{-1}\Delta(C).
\]

Consequently the latter equation proves the equivalence

\[
 \Delta(C)=B A(C)\ \text{for every }C
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \tau_{\mathrm{zero}}(C)=R\overline{A(C)}
 \ \text{for every }C.
\]

It does **not** prove equivalence with L351's weaker law on the final
optimizing physical lift. L355 §3 identified those two laws before
this audit; its text now distinguishes them explicitly.
Knowing the fully optimized law determines the restriction of
\(\Delta\) to that lift, not its values on the disk fibre.

### Exact quadratic countermodel to the claimed implication

Take \(a\in\mathbb C^2\), a real disk coordinate \(d\), and
\(\tau=(\tau_0,\tau_1)\in\mathbb C^2\). Define

\[
 A(a,d)=a,\quad e(a,d)=-\|a\|^2-d^2,
 \quad T=I,\quad \Delta(a,d)=(0,d),
 \quad D_+=\operatorname{diag}(3,1),
\]

and

\[
 4J_{a,d}(\tau)=e(a,d)-\tfrac12
 \|\overline\tau+\Delta(a,d)\|_{D_+}^2.
\]

This has exactly L355's square identity, an invertible tracking
corrector, a strictly negative disk block, an interior-valued
\(\Delta\), a unique zero optimizer, and a surjective
shape/tracking chart. At fixed \((a,d)\),
\(\tau_{\mathrm{zero}}=(0,-d)\). After maximizing also over
\(d\), the unique optimizer has \(d=0\) and
\(\tau_{\mathrm{full}}(a)=0\), a perfectly antiholomorphic law.
Nevertheless \(\Delta(0,d)\ne0\) when \(d\ne0\); no factorization
\(\Delta=B A\) exists.

This is a countermodel to the logical implication, **not** a Gau–Wu
counterexample or evidence against the observed Gau–Wu factorization.
The repair is to make L355's relation to L351 one-way sufficiency,
or explicitly retain the separate disk-annihilation hypothesis in
the converse. The raw fixed-physical-direction law remains exactly
equivalent to L354's factorization.

L354 §4's converse likewise needs its precise quotient specified:
surjectivity of the shape/tracking chart alone does not make an
arbitrary physical response descend through that chart. Its two
listed proof debts—disk annihilation and absence of an
\(\overline a\) term—are correctly stated and should not be collapsed
by an informal invariance-of-range argument.

## 3. Real versus complex orthogonality

The homogeneous metric and zero residual ranges are respectively

\[
 \mathcal G_- =\{(u,-\Gamma u)\},\qquad
 \mathcal G_+ =\{(v,\Gamma v)\},
\]

where \(\Gamma\) is **antilinear**. These are real-linear subspaces,
not complex-linear subspaces for the ordinary componentwise complex
structure. Their orthogonality is with respect to

\[
 g(r,s)=\operatorname{Re}
 (\langle r_-,s_-\rangle_{D_-}
   +\langle r_+,s_+\rangle_{D_+}).
\]

Their ordinary complex inner product is generally nonzero: choosing
\(v=iu\) gives a purely imaginary nonzero pairing. This does not
invalidate any norm-square cancellation. Both ranges are invariant
under the **signed** complex structure
\((u_-,u_+)\mapsto(-iu_-,iu_+)\), and can instead be treated as
complex subspaces using that structure and its induced Hermitian
product. L352/L355 should specify which sense of orthogonality is
intended. In particular, “linearity” of a joint physical/metric/zero
map means real linearity unless a complex structure is stated.

## 4. Keep shift and ordinary endpoint scalings explicit

L338's pure-zero identities are in shift coordinates:

\[
 h(S)q=-J_fk,\qquad h(S)^*p=-zk.
\]

L342's residuals, and the numerical implementations, use ordinary
Gau–Wu coordinates \(A=XSX^{-1}\), with
\(X=\sqrt2P_p+P_{\mathcal M}+P_q/\sqrt2\). Accordingly their
pure-zero endpoint residuals are actually

\[
 r_-=-L_-J_fk,\qquad r_+=-L_+zk,
\]

where

\[
 L_-=\sqrt2P_{\mathcal M}+P_q\quad\text{on }p^\perp,
 \qquad
 L_+=P_p+\sqrt2P_{\mathcal M}\quad\text{on }q^\perp.
\]

Thus \(d=-2L_+zk\), and the ordinary-coordinate corrector is
\(T\overline\tau=-2L_+zk_\tau\). Since
\(\Gamma L_-=L_+\Gamma\), all graph, endpoint-trace, invertibility,
and phase conclusions survive. L354 mentions a fixed diagonal later;
writing it at the first use of its equations (7)–(11) would avoid
mixing two coordinate conventions in identities labeled exact.

The full endpoint-trace cancellation itself is straightforward:
\((a_+)_p=Z_{pq}/3\) and
\(\overline{(a_-)_q}=-Z_{pq}/3\), so the metric terms cancel and
\(d_p=\overline{(F_1)_{pp}+(F_1)_{qq}}\).

- L353's reduction uses the valid analytic functional-calculus
  identity \(DK_s(A)[K_s(A)]=K_s'(A)K_s(A)\). Its paired-frame

  The \(-K_t\) coefficient cancels precisely as stated, provided

  \(S^*q=0\); it includes the necessary first-curvature reserve.
- L355's minimization is ordinary real least squares after the
  antiunitary change \(y=\Gamma u\). It requires no unproved
  physical shape factorization. The exact scalar/similarity identity
  still depends on L342's transferred gap identity; this audit does
  not replace that dependency with a new general proof.
- The common physical coefficient is genuinely undetermined by the
  gap identity. Adding any real physical quadratic \(q(C)\) to both
  \(F_C\) and \(4J_C\) leaves the gap unchanged. No residual-side
  symmetry can alone settle that coefficient's phase or sign.
- L338's optimized endpoint-flux positivity is a **sufficient**
  gate, not automatically equivalent to the scalar sign: L336 (3)
  has an additional nonpositive endpoint-diagonal-square reserve.
  L336 explicitly records this distinction; stronger “sole content”
  wording elsewhere should not erase it.

## 6. Scope audit against `goal.txt`

The goal requires a rigorous bound for every finite-dimensional
matrix and polynomial, or one rigorously certified ratio strictly
above two. L352–L355 do not supply either. Even a proof that
\(\widehat H_\phi\preceq0\) for every nondegenerate Gau–Wu model
would establish only a second-order statement along this equality
stratum.

Additional obligations remain:

1. **Local conclusion from jets.** Nonpositive Hessian alone does
   not imply a local bound (the scalar function \(2+t^4\) has zero
   Hessian at zero). Strictness transverse to all equality
   directions, control of neutral directions, and uniform local
   remainder/envelope arguments must be supplied.
2. **Degenerate strata.** Root collisions, vanishing tracking
   derivatives, support multiplicities, boundary limits of model
   parameters, and reducing direct sums are not covered by the
   nondegenerate formulas without additional arguments.
3. **Global bridge.** There is no audited theorem reducing every
   possible violation, or every first boundary point of a violating
   region, to these Gau–Wu models. Local maximality of a known
   ratio-two family does not exclude a disjoint region with a
   larger value.
4. **All dimensions and functions.** Any compactness, degree
   restriction, continuity argument, and equality classification
   used for that bridge must apply at the required scope. A fixed
   model spectral gap or finite set of matrix sizes is not that
   evidence.

`RESEARCH_STATE.md` already acknowledges sums and global
classification in its distant next-actions section. Its opening
“exactly one current mathematical gate” should therefore read
“one current local Hessian gate” and display the global obligations
nearby, rather than letting the active subproblem replace the goal.
