# An explicit polynomial preimage cancels the canonical cubic flag

## 1. Result (L230, 2026-07-24)

Retain L229's pure balanced equality colligation

\[
I-S^*S=VV^*=:E,\qquad I-SS^*=WW^*=:F,\qquad EF=0,
\]

and write

\[
B_1=W^*S^*V,\qquad R_1=B_1^*B_1.
\]

L229 proved pointwise that the cubic upper-gap endpoint of the
canonical repair belongs to the free-column response range, but left
open a bounded analytic selection through the rank jump.  That
selection is the following polynomial column:

\[
\boxed{
C_3^{\rm free}=3(S^*)^2VR_1.}                    \tag{1}
\]

It is perpendicular to the right defect, \(V^*C_3^{\rm free}=0\),
and satisfies

\[
\boxed{
[c^3]{\cal U}_{\rm can}
=4{\cal M}_S(C_3^{\rm free}),}                    \tag{2}
\]

where

\[
{\cal M}_S(C)=W^*{\cal G}_S(VC^*+CV^*)W.
\]

Consequently, use (1) as the cubic defect-factor/free-column
coefficient, take the corresponding metric coefficient
\({\cal G}_S(VC^*+CV^*)\), and insert its level-zero/range row in
L194's exact metric chart.  This cancels A172's complete cubic
upper-gap coefficient while retaining exact lower tightness and
nonnegative prescribed Stein slack.

The selection is uniformly removable at the repeated apex:

\[
\|C_3^{\rm free}\|\le3\|B_1\|^2.                 \tag{3}
\]

Thus L229's analytic-divisibility debt is closed without a
pseudoinverse or a constant-rank assumption.  This is a cubic
preparation theorem, not yet the full partial-flag metric sandwich:
the prepared quartic and later coefficients remain to be analyzed.

## 2. Lift of the polynomial column

The transfer Gram has the state-space lift

\[
VR_1V^*=ESFS^*E.                                  \tag{4}
\]

Therefore the Hermitian forcing generated by (1) is

\[
\begin{aligned}
F_3^{\rm free}
&=V(C_3^{\rm free})^*+C_3^{\rm free}V^*\\
&=3\{(ESFS^*E)S^2+(S^*)^2(ESFS^*E)\}.             \tag{5}
\end{aligned}
\]

Both (1) and (5) are universal polynomials in the colligation data.
In particular, no choice of singular vectors or range bases is
needed as \(B_1\) changes rank.

## 3. Four-word Stein coboundary

Let \(L_3\) be L229's ten-word canonical cubic forcing, so

\[
[c^3]{\cal U}_{\rm can}
=-4W^*{\cal G}_S(L_3)W.                           \tag{6}
\]

Define the four-word Hermitian polynomial

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
Z={}&(S^*)^3S+S^*S^3\\
&-(S^*)^3S^3(S^*)^2-S^2(S^*)^3S^3 .
\end{aligned}}                                    \tag{7}
\]

Reduction only by

\[
SS^*S=S,\qquad S^*SS^*=S^*,\qquad EF=FE=0
\]

gives the two exact identities

\[
\boxed{L_3+F_3^{\rm free}=Z-S^*ZS,}               \tag{8}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{FZF=0.}                                    \tag{9}
\]

Since \(S\) is pure, the Stein series telescopes:

\[
{\cal G}_S(Z-S^*ZS)=Z.
\]

Equations (8)--(9), together with \(WW^*=F\), imply

\[
\begin{aligned}
0
&=W^*{\cal G}_S(L_3+F_3^{\rm free})W\\
&=W^*{\cal G}_S(L_3)W+
  {\cal M}_S(C_3^{\rm free}).
\end{aligned}                                     \tag{10}
\]

Combining (6) and (10) proves (2), including its sign and physical
factor four.

## 4. Analytic insertion and scope

The canonical metric is an exact contraction metric before imposing
its upper bound.  First form

\[
X_3^{\rm free}={\cal G}_S(F_3^{\rm free}).
\]

Its lower endpoint is zero, and its Stein forcing is precisely the
linear change induced by the defect-factor coefficient (1).  Use the
level-zero/range row of \(X_3^{\rm free}\) as the cubic free-row
coefficient in L194, while keeping the prescribed Stein Schur
complement nonnegative.  L194 then supplies a unique exact
real-analytic metric; its cubic endpoint change is

\[
-4{\cal M}_S(C_3^{\rm free}),
\]

which cancels (2).  Equivalently, the higher-order positive
completion includes the quadratic term required by the perturbed
defect factor.  Higher chart terms cannot alter this cubic
coefficient.

Because (1) is polynomial and quadratic in \(B_1\), it remains
bounded on every rank-changing analytic path and vanishes on the
complete-delay stratum \(B_1=0\).  It therefore does not disturb the
leading delayed face considered in L228.  It can, however, change
quartic and later mixed faces; those must be recomputed for the
prepared chart rather than copied from the unprepared canonical
repair.
