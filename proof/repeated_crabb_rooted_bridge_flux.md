# Every rooted bridge splits into a retained channel and an ideal response

## 1. Result (L305, 2026-07-26)

Retain the balanced pure partial-isometry colligation

\[
I-S^*S=VV^*=:E,\qquad I-SS^*=WW^*=:F,\qquad EF=0,
\]

and put

\[
B_j=W^*(S^*)^jV,\qquad G_j=ES^jF=VB_j^*W^*.       \tag{1}
\]

Let

\[
\begin{aligned}
{\cal G}_S(X)&=\sum_{n\ge0}(S^*)^nXS^n,\\
\Phi(A)&=W^*{\cal G}_S(VAV^*)W,\\
{\cal M}_S(C)
&=W^*{\cal G}_S(VC^*+CV^*)W,\qquad V^*C=0.
\end{aligned}                                      \tag{2}
\]

For arbitrary words \(p,q\) in \(S,S^*\), consider one rooted
physical-bridge term

\[
T=pG_jq=pVB_j^*W^*q.                              \tag{3}
\]

There is an explicit perpendicular column \(C_{p,q,j}\) such that

\[
\boxed{
W^*{\cal G}_S(T+T^*)W
=\Phi(R_{p,q,j})+\Phi(R_{p,q,j})^*
+{\cal M}_S(C_{p,q,j}),}                          \tag{4}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{R_{p,q,j}=B_j^*W^*qpV.}                    \tag{5}
\]

If \(|p|\) is the word length, then

\[
\boxed{
\|C_{p,q,j}\|_F\le(|p|+1)\|B_j\|_F.}              \tag{6}
\]

More importantly, its state lift remains in the same two-sided
bridge ideal.  With \({\cal A}=\operatorname{Alg}(S,S^*)\), put

\[
\boxed{
C_{p,q,j}V^*
\in {\cal I}_j
:=\overline{{\cal A}G_j{\cal A}
             +{\cal A}G_j^*{\cal A}}.}            \tag{7}
\]

The closure in (7) only accommodates stable Stein sums; every finite
word instance is algebraic.

Formula (4) is constructive.  It replaces the unproved general
cyclic-excision assertion considered after L304.  A bridge-ideal
term has exactly two pieces:

1. the rooted copy-space quotient (5), transported by \(\Phi\); and
2. a gap-free Markov response with an ideal-preserving column.

For a relative commutator the rooted quotient cancels:

\[
\boxed{
[\ell,pG_jq],\quad \ell\in\{S,S^*\}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
W^*{\cal G}_S\bigl([\ell,pG_jq]+[\ell,pG_jq]^*\bigr)W
={\cal M}_S(C_{\rm rel}),}                        \tag{8}
\]

with \(C_{\rm rel}V^*\in{\cal I}_j\) and a finite linear bound in
\(\|B_j\|_F\).  Thus L304's 28-term certificate is one special case
of (8), not evidence for a needed identity
\({\cal I}\cap[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]=[\mathcal A,\mathcal I]\).
Terms rooted at \(G_j^*\) obey the adjoint formula obtained by
replacing \(pG_j^*q\) with \((q^*G_jp^*)^*\); its bound uses
\(|q|+1\).

Finally, L298's grade-\(k\) retightening metric and frame lifts belong
to the cumulative ideal

\[
{\cal I}_{\le k}={\cal I}_1+\cdots+{\cal I}_k.    \tag{9}
\]

Consequently the complete L299 six-term moving defect generated by
retightening directions \(1,\ldots,k\) also belongs to
\({\cal I}_{\le k}\), coefficientwise in its all-series \(c\)
dependence.  The response part of its rooted decomposition can
therefore be corrected without leaving that ideal.

L305 closes the **response-selection/module-stability** part of the
arbitrary-grade recurrence.  It does not prove that the retained
rooted terms have the required positive even margins.  That is now
the sole live algebraic gate for the partial-retightening induction.
L309 subsequently rules out global odd cancellation.  The valid
target is a bounded hereditary factor through the cumulative active
transfer row at odd grades, and a direct transfer-Gram face plus a
prior-transfer cost chargeable to an earlier retained margin at even
grades.  L310 subsequently closes the hereditary endpoint factor
termwise.  Its delay-compatible response normalization and the even
sign/margin remain open.

## 2. Explicit correction column

Write

\[
p=x_1\cdots x_r,\qquad
u_t=x_1\cdots x_{t-1},\qquad
v_t=x_{t+1}\cdots x_r,                            \tag{10}
\]

and put \(Q=I-E\).  The column in (4) is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
C_{p,q,j}={}&Qp^*q^*WB_j\\
&+\sum_{x_t=S^*}
 S^*v_tVB_j^*W^*qu_tV\\
&-\sum_{x_t=S}
 S^*u_t^*q^*WB_jV^*v_t^*V .
\end{aligned}}                                    \tag{11}
\]

Every term in the two sums begins with \(S^*\), while the first term
is projected by \(Q\).  Since \(V^*S^*=0\),

\[
V^*C_{p,q,j}=0.                                   \tag{12}
\]

All factors other than \(B_j\) in (11) are contractions or
isometric frames.  There are \(r+1\) terms, which proves (6).

Multiplication on the right by \(V^*\) exposes the bridge in every
summand:

\[
\begin{aligned}
(Qp^*q^*WB_j)V^*
 &=Qp^*q^*G_j^*,\\
(S^*v_tVB_j^*W^*qu_tV)V^*
 &=S^*v_tG_jqu_tE,\\
(S^*u_t^*q^*WB_jV^*v_t^*V)V^*
 &=S^*u_t^*q^*G_j^*v_t^*E .
\end{aligned}                                    \tag{13}
\]

This proves (7) directly, without a pseudoinverse, a rank projection,
or a cyclic-homology hypothesis.

## 3. Proof of the endpoint split

For Hermitian endpoint test \(Y\), let

\[
H_Y-SH_YS^*=WYW^*,\qquad
A_Y=V^*H_YV,\qquad
Z_Y=(I-E)H_YV.                                   \tag{14}
\]

Stein adjointness gives

\[
A_Y=\Phi^*(Y),\qquad H_YV=VA_Y+Z_Y.               \tag{15}
\]

The observability commutators are

\[
[H_Y,S]=-SZ_YV^*,\qquad
[H_Y,S^*]=VZ_Y^*S^*.                             \tag{16}
\]

Move \(H_Y\) only through the prefix \(p\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}(H_YT)
={}&\operatorname{tr}\{[H_Y,p]VB_j^*W^*q\}\\
 &+\operatorname{tr}\{p(VA_Y+Z_Y)B_j^*W^*q\}.
\end{aligned}                                    \tag{17}
\]

The \(A_Y\) term is exactly the rooted channel:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}\{pVA_YB_j^*W^*q\}
&=\operatorname{tr}\{A_YB_j^*W^*qpV\}\\
&=\operatorname{tr}\{Y\Phi(R_{p,q,j})\}.          \tag{18}
\end{aligned}
\]

Expand \([H_Y,p]\) by the Leibniz rule and use (16).  After one cyclic
rotation, the remaining terms are precisely

\[
\boxed{
2\operatorname{Re}\left[
\operatorname{tr}\{[H_Y,p]VB_j^*W^*q\}
+\operatorname{tr}\{pZ_YB_j^*W^*q\}
\right]
=2\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}
  (Z_Y^*C_{p,q,j}).}                              \tag{19}
\]

Because (12) holds, the adjoint formula for the response map is

\[
\langle Y,{\cal M}_S(C)\rangle
=2\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(Z_Y^*C).    \tag{20}
\]

Equations (17)--(20), for every Hermitian \(Y\), prove (4).

The same calculation gives the direct gap-free estimate

\[
\left|
\left\langle Y,\,
W^*{\cal G}_S(T+T^*)W
-\Phi(R_{p,q,j})-\Phi(R_{p,q,j})^*
\right\rangle
\right|
\le2(|p|+1)\|B_j\|_F\|Z_Y\|_F.                  \tag{21}
\]

Thus the response bound survives every rank collapse of \(B_j\).

## 4. Relative commutators need no excision theorem

Apply (5) to the two rooted terms in

\[
[\ell,pG_jq]=(\ell p)G_jq-pG_j(q\ell).            \tag{22}
\]

Their copy roots are identical:

\[
B_j^*W^*q\ell pV.                                \tag{23}
\]

They therefore cancel before applying \(\Phi\).  Subtracting their
explicit columns from (11) proves (8).  For a finite sum

\[
\sum_\nu\alpha_\nu
[\ell_\nu,p_\nu G_{j_\nu}^{(\ast)}q_\nu],
\]

the response-column norm is bounded by the corresponding finite
coefficient/word-length sum times
\(\sum_\nu\|B_{j_\nu}\|_F\).

This is the exact fact needed from L304.  The stronger abstract
claim

\[
{\cal I}\cap[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]
=[\mathcal A,\mathcal I]
\]

is neither used nor asserted.  Such relative-cyclic exactness is not
automatic in general algebras.

## 5. Stability of the physical retightening ideal

For L298's grade-\(k\) direction, put \(A_k=B_k^*B_k\).  Equation
(1) gives

\[
VA_kV^*=G_kG_k^*.                                \tag{24}
\]

Hence the orbit Gram
\(\Omega(A_k)={\cal G}_S(VA_kV^*)\) lies in
\({\cal I}_k\).  L298's special column \(D_k\) also has

\[
D_kV^*\in{\cal I}_{\le k},                       \tag{25}
\]

because its leading term contains
\(S^kG_k^*\), while every mixed summand contains
\(G_kF(S^*)^jE\).  Therefore

\[
R_k\in{\cal I}_{\le k},\qquad
F_k^{\rm rt}V^*,\ VF_k^{{\rm rt}*}
\in{\cal I}_{\le k}.                             \tag{26}
\]

The six terms in L299's moving defect are products of \(R_k\), or of
the frame lift in (26), by arbitrary state factors.  For example,
if \(H=D-V\),

\[
HF_k^*=(HV^*)(VF_k^*),\qquad
F_kH^*=(F_kV^*)(VH^*),\qquad
F_kF_k^*=(F_kV^*)(VF_k^*).                       \tag{27}
\]

Since a two-sided ideal is closed under those multiplications,

\[
\boxed{{\cal N}^{(\le k)}(c)\in{\cal I}_{\le k}.} \tag{28}
\]

Stein inversion also preserves the closed ideal termwise.  Applying
(4) to a word presentation of (28) gives an ideal-valued explicit
response correction and a finite rooted quotient.

L285's compulsory lower elimination also preserves the same module:
if \(R_n\in{\cal I}_{\le k}\), then
\(ER_nE\in{\cal I}_{\le k}\), and the parallel column chosen from
that corner has its state lift in \({\cal I}_{\le k}\).  The metric
generated by the perpendicular column in (11) remains in the ideal
after Stein inversion.  Hence the response selection, lower
elimination, and the next triangular transport are one closed
finite-grade recurrence once the rooted quotient is assigned.

This does not contradict L285's counterexample: L284's old
endpoint-null quartic gauge did not itself have the bridge-ideal
property (26).  The new stability statement starts from L298's
oriented physical directions.

## 6. Remaining recurrence gate

At each finite grade, apply (4) to the complete L285/L299 forcing:

\[
\text{upper forcing}
=\underbrace{\sum\{\Phi(R_\nu)+\Phi(R_\nu)^*\}}
 _{\text{rooted retained quotient}}
+\underbrace{{\cal M}_S(C_n)}
 _{\text{explicit ideal-preserving response}}.   \tag{29}
\]

The second term is now closed constructively and remains eligible
for the next recurrence step.  The induction still must prove that
the first term:

1. at every odd preparation grade is a bounded hereditary endpoint
   factor through the cumulative active transfer row, modulo the
   displayed response; and
2. at every even grade is the new two-ended transfer Gram plus a
   bounded prior-transfer cost dominated by the fixed
   partial-retightening margins.

L300 proves the stronger global-response statement at cubic order,
but L309 proves that this does not persist at quintic order.
L303--L304 prove the second assertion at quartic order.  L310
subsequently converts every rooted channel term in (29) to a literal
bounded hereditary endpoint factor and closes its Smith valuations.
The remaining gate is the delay-compatible normalization of L310's
polarized response columns and the sign/margin of the even factor
sum.  Computing another isolated coefficient would not close it.
