# The cubic-prepared canonical repair has positive quartic trace

## 1. Result (L231, 2026-07-24)

Retain L227--L230's balanced pure equality colligation

\[
I-S^*S=VV^*=:E,\qquad I-SS^*=WW^*=:F,\qquad EF=0,
\]

and put

\[
B_j=W^*(S^*)^jV,\qquad R_1=B_1^*B_1.
\]

Let \(P_{\rm can}(c)\) be the exact canonical repaired metric and
factor its positive Stein slack canonically as

\[
P_{\rm can}-\widehat T^*P_{\rm can}\widehat T
=D_{\rm can}D_{\rm can}^*,
\qquad D_{\rm can}(0)=V.
\]

Use L230's cubic column

\[
C_3=3(S^*)^2VR_1
\]

and define one exact analytic cubic preparation by

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde D(c)&=D_{\rm can}(c)+c^3C_3,\\
\widetilde P(c)&=P_{\rm can}(c)+
{\cal G}_{\widehat T(c)}
\left(\widetilde D\widetilde D^*
      -D_{\rm can}D_{\rm can}^*\right).
\end{aligned}                                     \tag{1}
\]

Thus \(\widetilde P-\widehat T^*\widetilde P\widehat T
=\widetilde D\widetilde D^*\succeq0\) exactly.  L230 gives zero
cubic upper endpoint.  If \({\cal U}_{\rm prep}(c)\) denotes the
physical upper-gap Schur endpoint of \(\widetilde P(c)\), then

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname {tr}[c^4]{\cal U}_{\rm prep}
={}&12\|B_2\|_F^2+32\|B_1\|_F^2\\
&+56\operatorname {tr}\!\left\{(B_1^*B_1)^2\right\}.
\end{aligned}}                                    \tag{2}
\]

In particular the trace is positive unless \(B_1=B_2=0\).  On the
complete first-delay stratum,

\[
\boxed{
B_1=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
[c^4]{\cal U}_{\rm prep}=12B_2B_2^*.}             \tag{3}
\]

Equation (3) is the proved grade-two instance of L228; the cubic
preparation vanishes there and does not alter it.

The full quartic matrix need not be positive on
\(\ker B_1^*\).  Rank-changing examples have small but reproducible
negative eigenvalues.  Hence (2) is a favorable pointwise
range/separation result, not yet the quartic metric bound.

## 2. First coefficient of the exact slack factor

Let \(H(c)\) be the uncorrected boundary slack and
\(G(c)=V^*H(c)V\).  The canonical repaired slack factor is

\[
D_{\rm can}=HV G^{-1/2}.
\]

Writing \(H=E+cH_1+O(c^2)\) gives

\[
\boxed{
D_1=(I-E)H_1V+\frac12V(V^*H_1V).}                 \tag{4}
\]

Therefore the new slack coefficients generated by (1) are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\delta H_3&=VC_3^*+C_3V^*,\\
\delta H_4&=D_1C_3^*+C_3D_1^*.                    \tag{5}
\end{aligned}
\]

If \(Y=\widetilde P-P_{\rm can}\), the variable Stein recursion is

\[
\begin{aligned}
Y_3-S^*Y_3S&=\delta H_3,\\
Y_4-S^*Y_4S
&=\delta H_4+A_1^*Y_3S+S^*Y_3A_1.                \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]

L230's coboundary says that the complete prepared cubic metric
coefficient is just its four-word witness:

\[
\boxed{[c^3]\widetilde P=Z.}                      \tag{7}
\]

This supplies a short polynomial input for the quartic calculation.

## 3. Scalar endpoint reduction

Let \(M_2=[c^2]\widetilde P\), and let \({\cal F}_4\) denote the
complete right side of

\[
[c^4]\widetilde P-S^*[c^4]\widetilde P\,S
={\cal F}_4.                                      \tag{8}
\]

It is obtained from:

1. the boundary slack minus its canonical Schur residual;
2. the factor cross term in (5);
3. every variable-operator product involving
   \(I,M_2,Z\); and
4. the exact ellipse coefficients through order four.

No Stein sum is needed for its scalar upper compression.  Purity
gives

\[
\operatorname {tr}\{W^*{\cal G}_S(X)W\}
=\operatorname {tr}X.                             \tag{9}
\]

The constant balanced upper gap is zero on \(F\) and equals
\(I+2E\) on \(F^\perp\).  Its restricted inverse is

\[
R=I-F-\frac23E.                                   \tag{10}
\]

Because the first metric coefficient vanishes, the first upper
Schur square occurs at order four.  Equations (8)--(10) give

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname {tr}[c^4]{\cal U}_{\rm prep}
=-4\operatorname {tr}
\left\{{\cal F}_4+M_2RM_2F\right\}.}              \tag{11}
\]

The factor four is the physical congruence on the left defect.

## 4. Exact word reduction

Expand (11) only with

\[
SS^*S=S,\qquad S^*SS^*=S^*,\qquad EF=FE=0.
\]

After cyclic trace collection, the left side of (11) has six
classes:

\[
\begin{array}{c|r}
\text{cyclic word}&\text{coefficient}\\ \hline
I&80\\
S^*S&-72\\
(S^*)^2S^2&-84\\
(S^*)^3S^3&8\\
(S^*)^4S^4&12\\
(S^*)^3S^3(S^*)^3S^3&56 .
\end{array}                                       \tag{12}
\]

The state lift of the right side of (2) reduces to the same list
except for

\[
8\{-I+2S^*S-(S^*)^2S^2\}.                        \tag{13}
\]

This residual has zero trace.  Indeed, if the common defect
dimension is \(m\) and the state dimension is \(n\), orthogonality
of \(E\) and \(F\) gives

\[
\operatorname {tr}I=n,\qquad
\operatorname {tr}S^*S=n-m,\qquad
\operatorname {tr}(S^*)^2S^2=n-2m.               \tag{14}
\]

For the last equality, \(E\subset F^\perp=\operatorname {ran}S\);
therefore \(S^*ES\) is a rank-\(m\) subprojection of \(S^*S\), and
\((S^*)^2S^2=S^*S-S^*ES\).  Equations (12)--(14)
prove (2).

## 5. Consequence for the ordered partial flag

Apply L222's semidefinite alternative on
\(\ker B_1^*\).  Every positive annihilator of the compressed free
endpoint map comes from a reducing colligation summand on which
\(B_1=0\).  On such a summand, (2) becomes

\[
\operatorname {tr}[c^4]{\cal U}_{\rm prep}
=12\|B_2\|_F^2.                                   \tag{15}
\]

Thus every separator that sees a nonzero \(B_2\) pairs strictly
positively with the quartic gap.  A separator supported where
\(B_2=0\) belongs to the next ordered flag and correctly sees zero
quartic coercivity.  Therefore there is no new **pointwise**
semidefinite obstruction at grade two.

What remains is the same kind of issue L230 solved at cubic order:
construct a bounded polynomial quartic redistribution through all
rank jumps.  Positive total trace alone cannot be substituted for
that selection.
