# Arbitrary finite Gau--Wu Hessian: full numerical falsification gate

> **Status and scope.**  This is a numerical result, not a proof of a
> dimension-uniform Hessian theorem.  It tests the complete normal
> quotient at ten irreducible Gau--Wu equality models in dimensions
> \(4,\ldots,8\).  No positive mode was found.  The next step is one
> arbitrary-degree defect-one identity, not another isolated-size
> calculation.

## 1. Result (L334/A281, 2026-07-26)

Let the ordered zeros of \(\phi=zf\) be

\[
 0,b_1,\ldots,b_{n-2},0.
\]

In the Takenaka basis, put

\[
 s_{ij}=(-1)^{j-i-1}
 \overline b_{i+1}\cdots\overline b_{j-1}
 \sqrt{(1-|b_i|^2)(1-|b_j|^2)}
 \quad(i<j),
\]

with diagonal \(b_i\), and set

\[
 A=X S_\phi X^{-1},\qquad
 X=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt2,1,\ldots,1,1/\sqrt2).
 \tag{1}
\]

The checker first verifies

\[
 W(A)=\overline{\mathbb D},\qquad f(A)=2E_{1n}.
 \tag{2}
\]

For generic distinct interior zeros, the real tangent generated by
unitary conjugacy, complex affine motion, and all zero motions has
rank

\[
 n^2+2n-2.
 \tag{3}
\]

Consequently the normal quotient has dimension

\[
 2n^2-(n^2+2n-2)=(n-1)^2+1.
 \tag{4}
\]

Two seeded models in every dimension \(4,\ldots,8\) were tested on
that **entire** normal quotient.  The largest optimized Hessian
eigenvalues were:

| \(n\) | sample 0 | sample 1 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | \(-1.59135\,10^{-3}\) | \(-3.54539\,10^{-5}\) |
| 5 | \(-2.31046\,10^{-5}\) | \(-4.00116\,10^{-4}\) |
| 6 | \(-5.86702\,10^{-5}\) | \(-1.97812\,10^{-4}\) |
| 7 | \(-4.01243\,10^{-6}\) | \(-1.36751\,10^{-6}\) |
| 8 | \(-2.42723\,10^{-6}\) | \(-1.60480\,10^{-8}\) |

Thus no positive mode or unexplained kernel appeared.  The final
\(n=8\) value is deliberately classified as numerically unresolved
by the conservative \(2\,10^{-7}\) reporting threshold.  It remained
negative and stable from 256 through 2048 angular nodes:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
N&\lambda_{\max}\\\hline
256&-1.60479969\,10^{-8}\\
512&-1.60479877\,10^{-8}\\
1024&-1.60479914\,10^{-8}\\
2048&-1.60479806\,10^{-8}.
\end{array}
\tag{5}
\]

The softness is explained by two interior zeros near the Crabb
collision, not by a detected positive direction.  This is strong
evidence for strict negativity at every fixed nondegenerate model,
but it is not a certificate over all zero configurations.

## 2. The general analytic-jet calculation

This experiment does not finite-difference thousands of Riemann
maps.  If \(E_i\) are an orthonormal normal basis, sample the simple
top support eigenvector \(v_\theta\) and reduced resolvent

\[
 R_\theta=\sum_{\lambda_j<1}
 {v_jv_j^*\over1-\lambda_j}.
\]

The polarized support coefficients are

\[
 s_i(\theta)=v_\theta^*H_i(\theta)v_\theta,\qquad
 t_{ij}(\theta)=
 \operatorname{Re}v_\theta^*H_i(\theta)R_\theta
 H_j(\theta)v_\theta.
\tag{6}
\]

With \(F_i(w)=wH_{s_i}(w)\),

\[
 \delta_i=\operatorname{Im}H_{s_i}-s_i',\qquad
 K_{ij}(w)=wH_{t_{ij}-\delta_i\delta_j/2}(w),
\]

the inverse-Riemann jets are

\[
 {\cal G}_i=E_i-F_i(A),
\]

\[
 {\cal H}_{ij}
 =-\frac{DF_i(A)[E_j]+DF_j(A)[E_i]}2
  +\frac{F_i'(A)F_j(A)+F_j'(A)F_i(A)}2
  -K_{ij}(A).
\tag{7}
\]

FFT Schwarz coefficients evaluate (7) by ordinary matrix
polynomials.  Moving all \(n-1\) Blaschke zeros gives a joint real
quadratic form in

\[
 (n-1)^2+1+2(n-1)
\]

variables.  Its zero-motion block is strictly negative in every
tested case.  Its Schur complement is the optimized normal Hessian
reported above.

## 3. Independent checks

1. At a complex \(3\times3\) model, all five jet eigenvalues agree
   with L332's closed symbolic formula within
   \(1.76\,10^{-13}\).
2. On the soft \(n=5\) eigenvector, direct Theodorsen maps followed
   by nonlinear optimization of all four Blaschke zeros gave
   coefficients \(-2.73\,10^{-5}\), \(-2.59\,10^{-5}\), and
   \(-2.69\,10^{-5}\) at steps \(0.004,0.002,0.001\), versus the
   analytic-jet value \(-2.31\,10^{-5}\).
3. Every model identity (2), tangent rank (3), first-variation
   cancellation, support gap, and zero-block sign is checked before
   a record is accepted.
4. A coarse full finite-difference reconstruction in dimension seven
   produced an apparent positive matrix eigenvalue, but direct
   evaluation on that eigenvector was negative at both refined steps
   (\(-4.48\,10^{-4}\) and \(-3.92\,10^{-4}\)).  It was rejected,
   not logged as a witness.  The analytic jet gives the complete
   Hessian and avoids this subtraction artifact.

## 4. What this changes

The cheap falsification requested after L333 has survived.  It is
now strategically credible that L332 is the first member of a
dimension-free defect-one identity rather than an order-three
accident.

The remaining proof target is to express the optimized form as

\[
 Q_\phi=-{\cal R}_\phi^*{\cal R}_\phi
\tag{8}
\]

on the quotient by affine, unitary, and zero motions, and to prove
that \({\cal R}_\phi\) is injective when the finite Blaschke zeros
are nondegenerate.  Formula (7) should be rewritten using
model-space reproducing kernels or the defect-one colligation, not
expanded separately in dimensions four, five, and so on.  Root
collisions belong to the ramified Crabb charts already treated by
L192/L329.
