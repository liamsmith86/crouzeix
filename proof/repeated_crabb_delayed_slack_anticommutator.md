## 1. Result and status (L228, 2026-07-24)

Retain L225--L227's balanced pure partial isometry

\[
I-S^*S=VV^*=:E,\qquad I-SS^*=WW^*=:F,\qquad EF=0,

and put

\[

E_1=S^*ES,\qquad

Let \({\cal K}_S(c)\) be the right-defect Schur residual of the
boundary metric's elliptic Stein slack.  L227's grade-one formula has

\[
\boxed{[c^2]{\cal K}_S(c)=E_1F+FE_1.}              \tag{1}
\]

The all-delay covariance conjectured in L225 is therefore equivalent
to the concrete original-state identity

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
B_1=\cdots=B_{k-1}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
[c^{2k}]{\cal K}_S(c)
=E_1F_{k-1}+F_{k-1}E_1.
\end{aligned}}                                     \tag{2}

Every coefficient below \(c^{2k}\) must also vanish.  Formula (2) is
still a **candidate arbitrary-grade identity**, not a theorem.

1. it is exactly equivalent to the deflated matrix face in L225;

The candidate has rank at most \(2m\).  It replaces the abstract

## 2. Further collapse of the grade-one face

L227 proves, with \(Q_2=(S^*)^2S^2\),

\[
[c^2]{\cal K}_S(c)=2F-Q_2F-FQ_2.                  \tag{3}
\]

Defect telescoping gives

\[
I-Q_2=E+S^*ES=E+E_1.                              \tag{4}

Because \(EF=FE=0\), rewrite (3) as

\[
\begin{aligned}
2F-Q_2F-FQ_2
&=(I-Q_2)F+F(I-Q_2)\\
&=E_1F+FE_1.
\end{aligned}
\]

This proves (1) without a new ellipse expansion.

## 3. Equivalence with the deflated grade-one face

\(S_{k-1}\) be the deflated colligation.  Its left defect is

The first right orbit is also retained.  Indeed, for

\[

\[
J\{S_{k-1}^*V V^*S_{k-1}\}J^*=E_1.               \tag{5}

new ansatz nor weaker than L225's covariance: it is that covariance
written entirely in the original state space.

## 4. Exact endpoint response in every grade

The response of the candidate in (2) does not require the unresolved
covariance proof.  Put \(r=k-1\) and

\[
W_r=S^rW,\qquad C_r=S^*VB_k^*.
\]

The delay relations give

\[
E_1F_r=C_rW_r^*,\qquad F_rE_1=W_rC_r^*.            \tag{6}
\]

They also make the first \(r+1\) left-orbit frames orthonormal and
give

\[
W_r^*S^nW=\delta_{rn}I\qquad(n\ge0).               \tag{7}
\]

For \(n<r\), this is wandering orthogonality.  At \(n=r\) it is the
no-loss identity.  For \(n>r\), cancel \(S^*W_j=W_{j-1}\) exactly
\(r\) times and use \(W^*S^{n-r}W=0\).

Let

\[
\Phi(Y)=W^*{\cal G}_S(Y)W
=\sum_{n\ge0}W^*(S^*)^nYS^nW.
\]

By (7), each term in (6) contributes only at \(n=r\).  Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi(E_1F_r)
&=W^*(S^*)^rS^*VB_k^*
=B_kB_k^*,\\
\Phi(F_rE_1)&=B_kB_k^*.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Phi(E_1F_{k-1}+F_{k-1}E_1)=2B_kB_k^*.}           \tag{8}
\]

Taking the state trace directly also gives

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname {tr}(E_1F_{k-1}+F_{k-1}E_1)
=2\|B_k\|_F^2.}                                    \tag{9}
\]

Thus a proof of (2) immediately proves L225's all-grade trace law and
the full matrix cost of L227's canonical repair.  Combined with the
boundary faces, the repaired first faces would be

\[
+B_k^*B_k\quad\hbox{and}\quad -12B_kB_k^*.         \tag{10}
\]

This is still a conditional consequence because (2) remains open.

## 6. Remaining proof debt

The correct next theorem is a **one-delay associated-graded
recursion**:

\[
B_1=\cdots=B_\ell=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
[c^{2\ell+2}]{\cal K}_{S}
=J[c^{2\ell}]{\cal K}_{S_1}J^*.                   \tag{12}
\]

Here \(S_1\) removes the first wandering layer, so its first
\(\ell-1\) transfer coefficients are \(B_2,\ldots,B_\ell\).
Iterating (12) proves (2).  The word audit shows that the required
cancellations lie exactly in the two-sided delay ideals (11), but a
finite rewrite is not a proof of the recursion for all orders.
The next attack should group zero-, one-, and two-terminal-crossing
words before expanding their scalar ellipse coefficients; another
fixed-grade jet would add evidence without closing the theorem.
