# The first early terminal reflection in the delayed axis gauge

## 1. Result (L217, 2026-07-24)

The lower-tight defect-frame gauge for a delayed elliptic face cannot
be generated by keeping the universal zero-reflection frame unchanged
until the first active transfer grade.

The first counterexample is the exact repeated length-five monomial
axis.  Let \(S\) be the scalar length-five shift, let \(V,W\) be its
endpoint defects, and use the balanced physical equality metric

\[
P=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,2,2,2,4).
\]

The only nonzero transfer coefficient is

\[
B_5=W^*(S^*)^5V=1.                                  \tag{1}
\]

Let \(d_5(c)\) be the balanced rank-one defect frame of the exact
elliptic-axis metric from `proof/crabb_elliptic_axis.md`.  Through
order ten it is

\[
\begin{aligned}
d_5(c)
={}&(1-2c^2+4c^4-8c^6+14c^8-30c^{10})V\\
&+(-2c+4c^3-6c^5+14c^7-26c^9)(S^*)^2V\\
&+(2c^2-6c^4+12c^6-24c^8+42c^{10})(S^*)^4V
+O(c^{11}).                                        \tag{2}
\end{aligned}
\]

The zero-reflection frame used in L214--L215 is

\[
d_{\rm zr}(c)=
\frac1{1+2c^2}
\left\{V+2\sum_{j\ge1}(-c)^j(S^*)^{2j}V\right\}.    \tag{3}
\]

Exact subtraction gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
d_5-d_{\rm zr}
={}&(-2c^8+2c^{10})V\\
&+(2c^5-2c^7+6c^9)(S^*)^2V\\
&+(-2c^4+4c^6-8c^8+10c^{10})(S^*)^4V
+O(c^{11}).
\end{aligned}}                                      \tag{4}
\]

Since

\[
(S^*)^4V=SWB_5,\qquad (S^*)^2V=S^3WB_5,
\]

the leading correction is

\[
\boxed{
d_5-d_{\rm zr}
=-2c^4SWB_5+2c^5S^3WB_5+O(c^6).
}                                                   \tag{5}
\]

Thus a \(B_5\)-dependent terminal reflection is already required at
frame order four, one order *before* the first active transfer grade.
The naïve induction

> use the zero-reflection frame through order \(k-1\), then add a
> correction involving \(B_k\)

is false.

L217 does not contradict L214--L216 or the proposed prepared-base
identity.  It identifies why a termwise frame lift is the wrong
inductive object.  The full exact-axis normalization must be grouped
before deflation, or replaced by a matrix Schur/Levinson generating
recursion.  L216 remains applicable after that grouping because it
transports the resulting retained endpoint response exactly.

## 2. Exact metric input

For length \(L=5\), the all-size axis theorem writes the exact
physical metric as

\[
p_j(c)=\frac{S_j(c)}{S_0(c)c^j},\qquad
S_j(c)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}
\operatorname{sech}((j+10n)(-\log c)).              \tag{6}
\]

Expanding (6) through \(c^{10}\) gives

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
j&p_j(c)\\ \hline

1&2-2c^2+2c^4-2c^6+4c^8-8c^{10}\\

\end{array}                                        \tag{7}
\]

The exact direct Riemann pullback is generated independently from
L125's theta/ODE recurrence:

\[
T_5(c)=\phi_c(C_6+cC_6^*).
\]

Substitution in

\[
P_5(c)-T_5(c)^*P_5(c)T_5(c)
=P^{1/2}d_5(c)d_5(c)^*P^{1/2}                      \tag{8}
\]

and balanced extraction from the first Gram column gives (2).  Direct exact
expansion verifies that the residual in (8) is the zero matrix
through degree ten.  No floating fit is used.

## 3. Why grades two and three did not reveal the obstruction

At the length-four monomial axis, the zero-reflection frame (3)
already agrees with the exact axis through order eight.  At length
three, the first discrepancy is the later pair

\[
+2c^3SWB_3-2c^5SWB_3
\]

used by L215.  Length five is the first case in which a future
terminal coefficient changes the frame *before* its own grade.

Consequently a grade-four calculation on
\(B_1=B_2=B_3=0\) must allow its fourth frame coefficient to depend
on \(B_5\).  A gauge depending only on \(B_4\), or a minimum-norm
choice at order four, is not axis-compatible on the full stratum.
This explains the failure of the first blind grade-four expansion and
gives a concrete correction to test:

\[
-2QSWB_5\quad\text{at frame order four}.            \tag{9}
\]

Equation (9) is forced by the length-five axis, but L217 does not yet
claim that it is the complete general grade-four gauge.
