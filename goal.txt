THE CONJECTURE

For every complex (n\times n) matrix (A) and every complex polynomial (p),

[
|p(A)|*2\leq 2\max*{z\in W(A)}|p(z)|,
]

where

[
W(A)={x^*Ax:x\in\mathbb C^n,\ |x|_2=1}
]

is the numerical range and (|\cdot|_2) is the spectral operator norm.

Define

[
R(A,p)=\frac{|p(A)|*2}{\max*{z\in W(A)}|p(z)|}.
]

The conjecture is (R(A,p)\leq2) universally. Be careful because some papers define the reciprocal ratio.

The constant (2) is known to be sharp. For example, the matrix

[
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\0&0\end{pmatrix}
]

with (p(z)=z) attains equality. Therefore, equality at (2) is not a counterexample; disproof requires a rigorously certified value strictly greater than (2).
